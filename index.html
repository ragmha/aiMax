    About sources, access & topic mapping

    AI Engineer includes public talks and workshops across all years. Shorts, promotional clips and multi-session event streams are excluded. Unavailable/private videos cannot be inventoried; hidden playlist entries are not counted as public talks.

    Topic matching uses publisher playlist membership and description terms, with title terms helping prioritize matches. Where those are unavailable, title-only suggestions are always flagged for review. This is rule-based metadata classification, not a human review or transcript summary. Unclear subjects stay in Unclassified topics. A talk may appear in several topics.

    Universe sessions without a public recording link to attendee-only details. Sign in to the attendee portal ↗. This site does not collect credentials or personal access tokens.

    Years use the Universe event date or AI Engineer publication date, not an inferred AI Engineer event year. Publisher playlists are linked in each talk's mapping details. YouTube loads only after you choose a recording; watch history stays in this browser.
